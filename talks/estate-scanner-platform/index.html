Stitching works on keys the ontology declares

1 · Scanner output
A scanner emits assertions with provenance.
{"s":"java:svc-payments/PaymentsController#create",
 "p":"is_a","o":"Interface",
 "props":{"method":"POST","path":"/payments"},
 "scanner":"java-code",
 "snapshot":"snap-2026-10-01-a1","native_id":"…"}
2 · Ontology type
Each type declares its identity keys.
Interface:
  extends: Component
  identity:
    strong: [[service_key, method, path_template]]
    weak:   [name]
Deployment:
  extends: Component
  identity:
    strong: [image_digest]
3 · Stitch rules
Simple rules first, escape hatch when needed.
- id: pod-to-build
  from: {type: Deployment, key: image_digest}
  to:   {type: Build, key: image_digest}
  action: attach
- id: gateway-route
  from: {type: Route, perspective: gateway}
  to:   {type: RestApi, on: [method, path]}
  action: relate
- id: fuzzy-names
  candidates: "<graph query>"
  resolver: cli://resolvers/name-match
Every link records rule, version, evidence and confidence. Re-running is safe.
